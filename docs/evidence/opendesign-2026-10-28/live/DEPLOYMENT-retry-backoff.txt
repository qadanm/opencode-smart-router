Why
---
Measured over the live incident: the gateway fails ~5.5% of requests, and
16.7% of turns still died terminally. The failures come in runs - the gateway
is slow for a stretch, and every immediate retry landed inside the same
stretch and died with it, leaving the turn stopped until the owner typed
continue.

A provider-scoped failure is refused before a model runs, so an attempt costs
no allowance. The router now backs off between attempts (5s, 10s, 20s, 40s,
five attempts) so a retry lands after the bad stretch passes. Model-scoped
stream faults keep the short path (immediate retry, cap 3), since those are
usually one-offs.

Probe evidence (all with the owner's authorization)
---------------------------------------------------
  20 small non-streaming probes : 19/20 ok (1 connection drop mid-response)
  8 x ~100K-token probes        : 8/8 ok, 2.7-7.9s
  12 concurrent probes          : 12/12 ok
  15 streaming probes           : 14/15 ok (1 x 503 workspace_billing_scope_
                                  unavailable - the live error reproduced)
  -> ~5% failure rate in controlled probes, matching the live rate. Size and
     concurrency do not drive it; the gateway's own latency does.
