Why

(99.2%), the cheap route became ineligible, and the router moved the work to

What the router does now
------------------------
When a session goes idle with its context past the safe-window line of the
cheapest variant of its model (the same eligibility margin the router
already applies: context + 20k must stay under 95% of the window), it
triggers that session's own compaction. The session then fits the cheap
provider again, and the existing least-cost routing returns it there on the
next prompt - no new switching logic.

Guard rails
-----------
  - Only on a successful idle (never mid-turn; a busy session is untouched)
  - At most one router-triggered compaction per session per ten minutes
  - Never while the router is paused or the provider is backed off
  - Compaction is OpenCode's own built-in mechanism, not a new one

Cost
----
A compaction on OpenDesign reads the context at cache-read prices under the
0.5x credit multiplier - roughly a thousandth of the cost of the switch it
prevents.
